Always know who still owes you

Open the app and the first thing you see is the money still owed to you, with every unpaid booking underneath it.

  • Paid, part-paid and overdue at a glance
  • Record a payment or mark it paid in one tap
  • No more scrolling through notes and WhatsApp

Get paid in one tap

Every invoice and reminder comes with a Pay here button. Your client taps it and pays by PayPal, Revolut or Stripe.

  • Send from your own Gmail or Outlook
  • Pay links by WhatsApp or text message too
  • No bank details typed out by hand

Chase payments without the awkwardness

When a client is late, ShowFee writes a friendly reminder for you, with the invoice and a Pay here button attached. You just tap send.

  • Polite, professional wording
  • A nudge on your phone when a payment is late

Invoices with your logo

Tap once and ShowFee builds a clean PDF invoice with your details, numbered automatically and ready to send.

  • Your logo and colours on every invoice
  • Bank details and payment links added for you
  • Spreadsheet export for your accountant
