Why I need you

Google only lets a new app go public after at least 12 people have tested it for 14 days in a row. I'm a working singer who built ShowFee to stop chasing payments in notes and memory, and now I'd love other performers to use it too. Your two weeks gets it onto the Play Store for everyone.
