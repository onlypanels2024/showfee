Delete your ShowFee account and data

ShowFee – Bookings & Invoices, by Gary Falzon
In short: your bookings, clients and money are only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes them. The only things ShowFee keeps online are for the optional booking link: your booking page, the requests clients send you, and your booking link sign-in. You can delete all of these yourself in the app, or ask us to.

Delete your booking link and the requests sent to it

  1. Open ShowFee and go to Settings › Your booking link.
  2. Tap Remove my booking link at the bottom, then Remove.

Your booking page and all the requests clients sent to it are deleted straight away. Your bookings in ShowFee stay on your phone.

Delete your ShowFee account

Your ShowFee account is the sign-in you made with Google for your booking link. To delete it:

  1. Email showfee.app@gmail.com with the subject Delete my ShowFee account.
  2. Send it from the Google account you signed in with, and include your booking link (for example showfee.app/book/your-name) if you have one.

Within 30 days we delete your sign-in account (your Google email address and account ID), your booking page and every request sent to it, and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Booking page (name, work days, busy dates, your questions)Deleted
Booking requests from clients (names, contact details, answers, messages)Deleted
Booking link sign-in (Google email address and account ID)Deleted
Your bookings, clients, payments and settings in the appNever sent to us. They stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall ShowFee (or Android Settings › Apps › ShowFee › Storage › Clear storage).
Backup or export files you savedStay wherever you saved them (for example your Google Drive). Delete them there.
ShowFee Pro subscriptionHandled by Google Play. Cancel it in the Play Store › Payments & subscriptions. Google keeps its own purchase records under its own policies.

We don't keep any other copies or backups of booking link data.

Sent a booking request to a ShowFee user?

Email showfee.app@gmail.com with the booking link, the date you asked for and your name, and we'll delete your request within 30 days.
